Common questions about Chief AI

What is Chief AI?

Chief AI is a Founder Intelligence OS for organizing company knowledge, reasoning through business objectives, evaluating evidence and preparing decisions.

How is Chief different from PromptOS?

Chief focuses on organizational intelligence and business judgment. PromptOS independently focuses on reusable AI instruction engineering. Neither product grants execution authority.

Does a Chief recommendation automatically execute a business action?

No. A recommendation is distinct from approval, execution and proof of an outcome. Founder Control Room remains a separate governance product for authorized handoffs.

Can I use it for my business?

The existing workspace supports goal planning and browser-local company knowledge. Private account access, durable synchronization and verified provider execution require separate activation and validation.
