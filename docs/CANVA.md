# Canva Operating Guide

Use Canva as the fast visual-production and distribution layer for approved Chief AI ecosystem content.

Canva adapts product truth into usable assets. It does not invent product capabilities, customer proof, financial results, or launch status.

## Best Role

Use Canva for:

- social posts and carousels;
- pitch and presentation visuals;
- launch graphics;
- product explainers;
- simple brand kits and reusable templates;
- campaign variations from approved source material.

## Required Inputs

Before creating assets:

1. identify the product, audience, channel, and objective;
2. use approved brand assets, copy, claims, and product screenshots;
3. confirm dimensions and export format;
4. distinguish live features from planned features;
5. preserve privacy and remove sensitive information from screenshots.

## Mode Handling

### `/garyvee`

Turn one real product insight into multiple useful audience-specific assets. Lead with value, clarity, and authentic founder voice rather than corporate fog.

### `lindymode`

Use durable brand elements, readable typography, strong hierarchy, and templates that can be reused without chasing every design trend.

### `redteam`

Check for false claims, exposed names or account data, misleading screenshots, unreadable mobile text, accidental secret leakage, copyright risk, and calls to action that lead nowhere.

### `l99`

Protect brand continuity, source provenance, campaign history, asset naming, versioning, and the relationship between an asset and the product truth it represents.

### `ooda`

- **Observe:** approved source, audience, channel, and current campaign state.
- **Orient:** message hierarchy, visual system, privacy, and proof.
- **Decide:** one core message and required asset set.
- **Act:** produce, review, export, and record source/version information.

## Project Guardrails

### Se’kret Bip

- Never use real teen journals, voice content, private names, or sensitive screenshots in marketing.
- Avoid clinical promises and unsupported mental-health outcomes.
- Preserve the cool-cousin, safe, expressive, scrapbook-inspired identity without making safety feel childish.

### Chief AI

- Do not market the static prototype as a secure private AI control room until access control, private storage, and real model execution exist and are verified.

### Juss Beautiful Hair

- Use real product details, pricing, availability, and policies.
- Do not imply authenticity, luxury affiliation, results, or inventory that has not been verified.

### L99

- Lead with user outcomes such as story creation, continuity, and production workflows rather than dumping internal engine jargon into every asset.

## Deliverable Requirements

Each asset set should include:

- audience and objective;
- source copy or decision record;
- dimensions and channels;
- editable template link or file reference;
- export names;
- version/date;
- alt text or accessibility copy where relevant;
- claims requiring verification;
- approval status.

## Prohibited Behavior

- fabricated testimonials or metrics;
- unapproved logos, likenesses, or copyrighted material;
- sensitive screenshots;
- tiny mobile text masquerading as information design;
- making fifteen visual variants before the core message is settled.
