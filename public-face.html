Getting started · Chief AI

Start with one clear goal.

  1. Define the outcome. In the workspace, add a goal, project, and definition of done. Chief prepares a bounded plan you can review.
  2. Keep your work portable. This prototype keeps goals and drafts in this browser. Export a copy when you need to keep or move your work. Use a private device for sensitive material.
  3. Choose the next step. Explore the library, organize company knowledge, or build a draft. Plans and drafts do not execute actions on your behalf.

Workspace access follows the application's existing access rules. Cloud synchronization and paid delivery are not enabled by this introduction.

Chief AI · Public product definition

Founder intelligence that keeps its boundaries.

Chief AI turns founder intent, company context, prompts, workflows, research, and decisions into portable, versioned operating intelligence.

Direct answer

Chief AI helps founders turn goals into bounded plans with evidence requirements, constraints, rollback, and one clear next gate, while keeping company knowledge portable across AI providers.

What it does

  • Starts from the founder outcome instead of a provider.
  • Structures decisions, workflows, prompts, and research as reusable company intelligence.
  • Separates drafts, tested assets, and approved operating knowledge.
  • Keeps recommendation, authority, execution, and proof as different states.

Authority boundary

Chief AI can reason, synthesize, recommend, and prepare bounded plans. It does not gain execution authority merely because a model or repository check succeeds. Founder Control Room remains the governed execution, approval, evidence, and publication authority for portfolio actions.

Current product truth

The current application is a prototype with real browser-local capabilities. Private authentication, encrypted synchronization, durable shared persistence, and broader provider execution remain separate claims unless independently verified.

The recorded ChatGPT Site identity is known, but this repository does not independently claim current publication state, editor-account binding, custom-domain state, or deployment equivalence.
