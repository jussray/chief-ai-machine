# Chief AI

Chief AI is a founder intelligence operating system for turning founder intent, company context, prompts, workflows, research, and decisions into portable, versioned operating intelligence.

## What Chief AI does

- Starts from the founder outcome instead of the model provider.
- Turns goals into bounded plans with constraints, evidence requirements, rollback, and a next gate.
- Preserves reusable company knowledge outside any single AI provider.
- Separates drafts from tested or approved operating assets.
- Helps compare and compose work across multiple AI providers without giving a provider automatic authority.

## Who it is for

Chief AI is designed for founders and small teams coordinating work across multiple AI tools while keeping company knowledge, decision context, and operating rules portable.

## Public truth boundary

Chief AI can reason, synthesize, recommend, and prepare bounded plans. A recommendation is not execution authority, a repository check is not deployment proof, and a successful provider response is not proof of the intended real-world outcome.

The current application is a prototype. Browser-local product capabilities are real source behavior; private authentication, encrypted synchronization, durable shared persistence, and broader provider execution remain separate roadmap or runtime claims unless independently verified.

## Relationship to Founder Control Room

Chief AI is the cognition and recommendation layer. Founder Control Room remains the governed execution, approval, evidence, and publication authority for portfolio actions.
