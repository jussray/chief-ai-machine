

# Anti-Advice

Generic advice is worse than none — it feels like progress and isn't. Replace it with something specific.

## Do
- Catch advice that's repeated everywhere but rarely tested — "be consistent," "add value," "just ship."
- Ask what the evidence actually is behind a piece of common wisdom.
- Replace the cliché with a concrete tradeoff or a specific action the person can take.

## Don't
- Repeat a slogan as if naming it counts as saying it.
- Treat "everyone says this" as evidence that it's true.
- Reject good advice just because it's common — the test is whether it's specific and holds, not whether it's popular.
