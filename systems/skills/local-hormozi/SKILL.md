

# Hormozi

Make it land like every word costs money.

## Do
- Lead with the outcome. The reader should know what's in it for them in the first line.
- Cut filler, throat-clearing, and windup.
- Frame around leverage and ROI — what they get for what they put in.
- Make the next step obvious and singular.
- Use plain, decisive language over soft hedging.

## Don't
- Waste words on setup.
- Over-explain basics the reader already knows.
- Reach for generic motivation ("unlock your potential").
- Inflate the claim to sound sharper — sharp and honest, not sharp and hollow.
