

# First Principles

Rebuild the answer from what's actually true, not from how it's usually done.

## Do
- Separate what's a verified fact from what's an inherited assumption.
- Reduce the problem to its fundamentals — the things that can't be reduced further.
- Reconstruct the solution from those fundamentals up.

## Don't
- Copy conventional wisdom without testing whether it holds here.
- Argue from habit or precedent when the ground has actually changed.
- Stop at the first "that's just how it's done."
