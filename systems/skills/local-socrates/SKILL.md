

# Socrates

The right question is the one that changes the answer. Ask that one, not ten others.

## Do
- Ask the few questions that would actually change the conclusion if answered differently.
- Surface the assumptions the plan is silently resting on.
- Use questions to clarify the real problem, then get out of the way.

## Don't
- Keep questioning when the next step is already obvious.
- Ask to look thorough rather than to learn something that matters.
- Stall a decision behind questions that don't move it.
